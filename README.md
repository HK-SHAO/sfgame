## 怎么玩 · How to Play

- 轻点地面放热源，长按放冷源<br>
  <i>Tap to place a hot source, long-press for a cold one.</i>
- 空气遇热上升、遇冷下沉，冷暖之间的压力差就是风<br>
  <i>Warm air rises, cool air sinks — the pressure between them becomes wind.</i>
- 风托起纸飞机，越过山谷、绕过山峰，抵达绿色旗帜即通关<br>
  <i>Your wind lifts the paper plane over valleys and ridges to the green flag.</i>
- 点击已放置的源可随时移除<br>
  <i>Tap a placed source to remove it.</i>
- 没有敌人，没有倒计时——只有风，和你想去的方向<br>
  <i>No enemies, no countdown — just wind, and wherever you want to go.</i>

## 20 段旅程 · 20 Journeys

四个篇章：长风、焚风、烈风、罡风。
<i>Four chapters: Long Wind, Foehn, Gale, and Squall.</i>

从第一缕风到越过天堑——燃烧的篝火、顺坡滑落的冷气、摇头的风扇、起伏的潮汐，还有初霜与灼原的极端气候。
<i>From the first breeze to the great divide — crackling fires, cold air spilling downhill, swaying fans, breathing tides, and the extremes of frost and scorching heat.</i>

风不是魔法，而是温度的翻译。每一关都是一场可以亲手摆弄的物理小实验。
<i>Wind is not magic; it is temperature, translated. Each level is a small physics experiment you can toy with by hand.</i>

## 氛围 · Atmosphere

暖黄手绘世界，安静的钢琴与风铃。手机、平板、桌面浏览器皆可游玩。
<i>A hand-drawn world of warm cream light, with gentle piano and wind chimes. Playable in any browser on phone, tablet, or desktop.</i>

## 技术栈 · Tech Stack

TypeScript · Lit · WebGL · WASM 数值内核（Moonbit）· Vite · Bun

## 运行 · Run
